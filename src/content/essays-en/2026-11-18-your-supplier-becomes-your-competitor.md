Not long ago, Fortune reported that OpenAI had shut down its robotics team. The researchers have moved into its core research. OpenAI's own explanation is that its general-purpose models, including GPT-6 Astra, can be used directly on robots. In a simulated test, an Astra agent beat the best purpose-built robotics models.

Robotics is therefore no longer a separate problem requiring separate models at OpenAI. It is an application of the general model.

We see the same pattern everywhere. OpenAI has its own products for healthcare and finance, and its finance product is aimed squarely at the work junior bankers do. Anthropic has Claude for Legal and ready-made agents for finance. In May, Anthropic launched a services company with Blackstone and Goldman Sachs, worth $1.5 billion, that puts Claude and its own engineers directly into businesses.

In other words: the labs have started selling the work itself.

Here is what I think is coming: the labs have something nobody else has. They see what millions of users and businesses use the models for, what works and where the money is. They own the computing power. And they have internal models that are better, probably much better, than the ones we get. METR estimates that Anthropic is now improving its models about twice as fast as before, probably mostly because it uses AI in the work.

Amazon did something similar on a small scale. It saw what sold on its own marketplace, made its own products and attracted the attention of the European Commission. Now the same could happen with all cognitive work.

As long as everyone can buy roughly equally good models, it pays the labs to sell to everyone. If the gap between what they have internally and what we get grows large enough, the calculation changes. Why rent an AGI to an accounting firm when you can own the accounting firm?

I think we will see the beginnings of this within a couple of years. First software, then services, then entire companies.

There are brakes on this. Three or four labs are competing fiercely, open models lag only a few months behind, and competition authorities are watching. But the first two only work while the gap is small. And right now, the gap between the leading and open models is widening again.

To those building your business on one of these platforms: who else can see what you are doing? What will you do on the day Anthropic or OpenAI launches a product that competes with yours?
